About Our Department

A Legacy of Innovation

Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, Bidar (GNDECB), established in 1980, is a premier institution dedicated to academic excellence. Our AIML Innovation Hub serves as the neural center for cutting-edge research, industrial collaboration, and student-led breakthroughs in Artificial Intelligence.

• Promote research and consultancy activities.
