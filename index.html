A Rich History of Excellence

Founded in 1980 under the visionary leadership of the Prabandhak Committee, GNDECB was established to provide quality technical education to the students of North-East Karnataka.

Our 0.5 million sq. ft. sprawling campus houses state-of-the-art infrastructure. GNDECB is Affiliated to VTU Belagavi, approved by AICTE, and ranked NAAC A+.
